To be honest, I started this project maybe a year ago and had terrible results until recently.  More to follow on this later in the article.
  I like the idea of an offline comm's outside of the realm of "HAM" and doesnt require me giving money and personal information to some public database just to be "licensed" to use.

What is LoRa?

<u>**My build considerations.**</u>  
**Coverage.**  Which flavor of software is widely supported in my area? Mestastic vs Meshcore vs Reticulum.  
**GPS.**  I want the capability of sharing locations between nodes privately without advertising it to the public.  
**Antenna.**  Just like any budget radio i've purchased in the past, the antenna that comes with the radio is probably complete dog shit.  Does it work?  Sure.  Is there a better alternative?  Absoultley. 

Hardware.  
LoRa Board: Heltec V3  
GPS: GY-NEO6MV2 NEO-6M GPS Module  
Antenna: Muzi Works 915Mhz Whip Antenna  
Case: 3D printed with PETG filament








the LoRa mesh relies heavily on public support...I dont care if you only want to talk to family and friends in emergencies; if you dont have fellow nerds running the same software, you will never talk farther than a mile or two.
